# W01 Chainaxe player-copy review: 0.0.16

This record documents the Web-owned Chainaxe copy proposed for pending preview 0.0.16. The two cards share the official family name `突擊鏈斧` as their title and are distinguished by `Mk IV` and `Mk XII`. Official EN and zh-TW source strings remain byte-exact in the generated JSON; the weapon cards do not repeat an official-text block.

The attack segment labels summarize the roles displayed by the game for each segment. They do not claim to reproduce the full action chain or calculate damage per segment. `60%` and `80%` are comparison inputs for Source-defined visible stat values, not obtainable or drop limits. Attack power and sawing power are not final health damage.

## Source bindings and display

The Web cards bind to the pinned Source package at commit `285a06c8939ac505ac48e4d0e93b939a2b959211`, revision `3af04f0212a88e6b29f91f397178dd6d22c0c27d40566088eca8f8f06d4845c1`, manifest SHA-256 `3140d7c5f8f49b2cd9b8574819159079d8542cf165d870af96890ca8efe063d6`. The `weapons.json` presentation rows retain their exact Source `claimRefs`; each of the 38 metrics has one 60% and one 80% claim, for 76 numeric claims total. The claims bind the mark, attack mode/profile where applicable, Source stat family, property, unit, raw value, and Source UI `displayValue`/`displayFormat`.

The visible rows cover Damage (2 per mark), Armor Penetration (5 for Mk IV and 6 for Mk XII), Defence (3 per mark), Sawing (5 for Mk IV and 4 for Mk XII), and Mobility (4 per mark). All 19 metrics per mark are shown; an unknown armor class or missing player label fails the build rather than dropping a row. `resistant` is labeled 不屈, and `disgustingly_resilient` is labeled 被感染, matching the Source localization keys and fixed Mods glossary.

Raw values remain distinct from displayed values. For example, sprint stamina cost retains raw `0.975` / `0.925` and displays `0.97` / `0.93` under the game UI's two-decimal formatting. Armor ratios display as percentages; signed mobility rows preserve their display sign. The UI formatting test covers these conversions. The comparison describes pinned profile parameters, not per-segment health damage, a complete action chain, or an obtainable rating range.

Copy review uses `推擊` for the game's push action. The stamina modifier row is shortened to `耐力` with `+4.00`; its `stamina-point` unit remains typed in the source claim and generated JSON, while the player label avoids repeating the unit.

## Version and review state

The current candidate is `0.0.16`, with Source inputs pinned as above. Web authoring holds only player labels, attack-role layout, and claim references; it does not duplicate the 76 metric values. The package remains `pending-preview`, publication-ineligible, and pending human review.
