Introducing the Sister Novitiate

The Sisters Novitiate are young recruits to the Adepta Sororitas, the fighting force of the Adeptus Ministorum. Warriors in training that are yet to earn the powerful Power Armour and Bolter weapons of the God-Emperor. But they are no pushovers as they have the training and specialised equipment to combat the ever growing threats on Atoma.

As the eighth class of the game, it was important for us to craft something that felt different from the other classes. Both on a gameplay level, but also on how they look and feel as class. For this class we wanted to dig deeper into the Faith in the Emperor and the Imperial Creed as thematic pillars. We already have the Zealot that cover some of that ground, but while the Zealot is more unbound fanaticism, the Sisters Novitiate brings a more ordered and militaristic feel to this aspect of the Imperium.

While investigating what we wanted to do with the class, we kept coming back to the Sisters Novitiate. Not only did it fit for the aspects mentioned above, but the level of combat experience and gear they bring fit perfectly for a Darktide class. While the Power Armour clad Sororitas might be a bit more iconic in the Warhammer 40,000 universe, what the Novitiate brought to the table made a lot of sense to us, and we felt it fit the more grounded combat of the game better.

We have had such a blast turning this class from concept into a full fledged experience that is soon to be released into the game!

Blazing unto the Battlefield

To make sure the class felt distinct, we have crafted it around an even more tactical approach than the other classes in the game. The class is at its best when you think ahead and plan for the next combat scenario, and positioning and timing will be key to play it well.

“It’s always fun to break the rules we have set up for the game and make something new” ~ Victor

You will also have access to builds that let you play pure support, with tools from granting temporary health to the team, to handing out buffs and debuffs to craft the perfect combat scenario for you and your team.

One thing we knew we had to pay close attention to is the thematic overlaps shared between the Novitiates and the Zealots. We wanted to make sure the two classes would feel distinct in their own right, so we have worked on minimizing any overlap in how they play and the role they have in the strike team. In the latest balance patch, we refined the role of the Zealot class with this in mind too.

They have their own role to play in the team, even if they do share that overlap in dedication to the God-Emperor!

“A lot of the mechanics are tied to making the right decision, at the right time but also at the right place.” ~ Teodor

As part of her arsenal you will find new weapons, talents and not to mention her purely unique mechanic - the Acts of Faith.

Acts of Faith

Unlike other classes the Sisters Novitiate are able to choose from six divine and devastating Acts of Faith as opposed to blitz and combat abilities. They can pick up to two Acts that they bring into combat for a mission, and the choice on what to bring is fully up to you.

These Acts draw upon reserves of Faith, a resource unique to them. Faith is spent to activate the Acts of Faith and it regenerates over time.There are also other ways to regain faith through Devotions and Pledges within their talent tree, each shaping how Faith is both accumulated and put to use.

Each Act has a different Faith cost, so a big part of the class design revolves around how to manage this, and what Acts to bring in the first place. Some of the cheaper ones can be used repeatedly, while the ones that cost more Faith will have to be carefully used at the right time so you don’t end up running out of resources.

“I can’t wait to see how players approach the Acts of Faith, it’s such an open ended system. Will you use the cheaper ones repeatedly or go for the more cost intensive ones?” ~ Victor

We will dive deeper into the Acts of Faith in a future Feature Highlight, but let’s have a quick look at them now before moving on!

The Neural Whip lets you attack enemies in a wide arc, staggering them. You will also be able to activate it repeatedly, resulting in a chain of attacks, as long as you have enough Faith available.

They can plant the Icon of Purity, a banner that buffs every team member who can see it. This means placing it in the right spot is key.

The Auto-Broadcaster is deployed by the Sister Novitiate, and covers an area in a cone shape, blasting condemning sermons at the enemies and pushing them back. Perfect for battlefield control.

The Novitiate can also make use of the Sanctification Grenade, throwing it and dousing all enemies in the area of impact in holy water that applies Sanctification, a new debuff that deals more damage to tougher targets. The Grenade can also be upgraded through the talent tree to for example create a zone that blocks line of sight.

Shield of Aversion is an Act that lets you utter a prayer that applies a protective buff to allies you’re aiming at in front of the Novitiate.

Healing can also be performed with the Chirurgeon’s Tools Act. This heals a chosen ally a fair amount, but the health granted by this Act is only temporary, gradually draining down.

“I’m looking forward to seeing the different combinations of talents and Acts of Faith people will build around” ~ Gunnar

As said, you bring two of these Acts into a mission, and since they all cost different amounts of Faith to use, figuring out the perfect combo and how and when to use them will play a big part in making the class shine.

Arms of the Ecclesiarchy

Faith is not the only weapon the Sisters bring to the conflict though. The class comes with six fully unique weapons. We will go into depth on all of these in the next Feature Highlight, but let’s go through them at glance today.

Let’s start with the Condemnor Stake Thrower, a semi-automatic crossbow that fires blessed stakes. A weapon crafted to perfection in the fight against rogue Psykers. You can even use Faith to empower the next shot you make with the Stake Thrower to apply Sanctification to the enemy. If that was not enough, it can also pin enemies to walls if they are close enough when hit. We are very excited for this one!

“The Stake Thrower is a definitely a favourite for many people in the team” ~ Gunnar

She can also wield the Ministrorum Hand Flamer, a lighter and more mobile version of the Flamer. As part of its Special Attack, the Hand Flamer can also fire a more powerful, high pressure flame, by expending more fuel.

For close combat encounters the Sister Novitiate have several new weapons to choose from. She can charge in with the Penitent Eviscerator, a huge chainsword that digs into enemies. We really wanted this version of the Eviscerator to feel brutal, and we can’t wait for you to get your hands on it.

Another heavy hitter is the two-handed Mace of the Righteous. It has a burning incense bearer inside of it, and as you swing it in melee, the flames are stoked and start setting enemies on fire. It can also create a smoke cloud that blocks line of sight.

If more elegant weapons are your style, you can go into battle with the Novitiate Sword and Shield combination. The Sword can deal quick damage with slashes, while the Shield stops enemies’ ranged attacks and can be used to shield bash. Like with the Stake Thrower, you can spend Faith for a special move with the Shield to make its Blocks and Pushes better.

Finally you can also wield the Novitiate Sword by itself, forgoing the protection of the shield, and opting for a more duelist approach. You can bless this weapon’s attack with Faith too. Finally for this one, the heavy attack functions as a block. If you played the Grail Knight career for Markus Kruber in Vermintide 2 and get a familiar feeling from using this, know that this was very intentional! Our way to pay homage.

But that is not all! As part of the free update coming out at the same time as the class, we will introduce the Meltagun! We know this is something players have been longing for in the game for a long time.

Firing a ray of superheated energy that ignites the air, it’s an extremely potent short range weapon. And while it is iconic in the arsenal of the Adepta Sororitas, we felt that we couldn’t add it to the game without letting the Veteran in on the fun too, so this will be a shared weapon between the Novitiate and the Veteran.

Crafting your own Sister Novitiate

As with all classes, while the talent combinations you pick are up to you, we have designed the talent tree with three archetypes in mind. One is leaning more into being a ranged combat backliner, calling shots on the tactical level. Here we focus on target priority and it is great setting up the team for victory against special enemies and bosses. It also helps out the team with healing.

The second archetype leans more towards a defensive build; striding into combat with the Novitiate Sword and Shield, commanding the flow of combat. Here we focused more on reviving and being the anchor that holds the team together.

Finally, for those of you that just wanna pick up that Penitent Eviscerator and bring the Emperor’s judgement up close, you can pick the archetype that does just that. Here you can focus your abilities on yourself, and you can even use your Faith as a shield, letting you absorb damage as long as you have enough of the resource left.

“I want to see how players engage with a more supportive class since Darktide is such a high energy game. This class is a bit more pulled back on that scale, it is its own thing in that” ~ Teodor

On the character creation side we have added some extra things. The Sisters Novitiate are all women, so instead of choosing between body types we have added a special body type just for them. This type is more muscular than the normal body type, representing the vigorous training these warriors go through. You can pick between this new one and the standard one.

We also added unique faces, hairstyles, hair colours and face tattoos, to give plenty of customisation to the Sister. The class also comes with four distinct personalities to pick from, showcasing different archetypes within the Adepta Sororitas. Though each is an unflinching warrior of faith, they still have their own burdens to bear.

Wrapping it up!

We are beyond excited to see what all of you will think when you get your hands on the class on December 7th. We will keep rolling out the Feature Highlights during the time leading us there, going into more detail on the Acts of Faith, the Weapons and the talents - there is just so much we can fit into one blog post after all!

That leads us to the end of this blog, that’s all for today!
