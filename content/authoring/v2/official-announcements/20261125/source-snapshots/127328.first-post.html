The Lord of Decay has blessed his most beloved followers with hideous tentacles. So imbued are they with his foul pestilence that they explode when shot in the head.

Warband!

Enemies have been imbued with foul warp energy, causing them to sprout tentacles. These enemies explode when shot in the head. Thankfully, Command has granted the Strike Team extra ammunition and grenades, as well as Incendiary Ammunition for these missions!

Starting today until November 26, complete missions with the ‘Mutated Horrors’ condition to make progress in the event!

The rewards will be Ordo dockets, Plasteel and Diamantine. Those who complete the last tier of the event will receive a cosmetic insignia.
