This Feature Highlight covers the new weapons coming with the Sisters Novitiates in the next update!

Warband!

Heresy Shall Be Met With Fire and Righteous Wrath as the Sisters Novitiate Arrive in Warhammer 40,000: Darktide on December 7th!

In case you missed it, check out the reveal trailer here.

This is part of an ongoing series of feature highlights focused on the upcoming DLC class: The Sisters Novitiate.

In our previous Highlight we went into an overview of the class and the design behind it. Today, we’ve interviewed our game designers Matteo and Erik about the new weapons coming with the Sister on December 7!

“Hello, I’m Erik, one of the Combat Designers on Darktide. We work on Weapons and Combat Design in general, and anything related to it. We’re also the best memers and emoji makers in the company.” ~ Erik

“I’m Matteo, also a Combat Designer, also a big meme enjoyer.” ~ Matteo

Choice of Weapons

Whenever we work on a new class, one of the big questions we always have to solve is what kind of weapons they will wield! As per usual, we take a look at every relevant source we can to find what we can add to their arsenal, such as official Novitiate miniatures and the larger armoury of the Adepta Sororitas.

This informed our choices not just of what new weapons to make for the class, but also which existing weapons to give them access to!

“We aimed at creating a well rounded offering suited for all different types of engagement, that also played great together with the unique and new weapons added with the class.” ~ Matteo

Here’s the list of the already existing weapons that the Sister Novitiates will be able to use:

Melee

  1. Assault Chainsword

  2. Assault Chainaxe

  3. Heavy Eviscerator

  4. Heavy Sword

  5. Combat Axe

  6. Tactical Axe

  7. Shock Maul

  8. Crusher

Ranged

  1. Infantry Autogun

  2. Braced Autogun

  3. Vigilant autogun

  4. Shredder Autopistol

  5. Purgation Flamer

  6. Combat Shotgun

  7. Double-Barreled Shotgun

  8. Huntsman’s Shotgun

  9. Stub Revolver

While Boltguns and Bolt Pistols are iconic weaponry for the Sisters of Battle, Novitiates who have not yet been accepted as full Sisters are not permitted to use bolt weapons until they have proved themselves worthy in the eyes of the God-Emperor – or, more accurately, in the eyes of their stern superiors. As such, the Sisters Novitiate class in Darktide do not have access to them.

With that said, let’s get into the brand new weapons!

Mace of the Righteous

mace1920×1059 601 KB

This most holy of maces has a censer for a head, which contains sacred coals and incense that are always burning. While normally they are subtle embers with only some faint incense smoking billowing from the censer, striking enemies with the Mace will stoke the fires inside it!

This is shown visually in the fires of the weapon, as well as in the UI with a three-segmented bar that fills up on hit, and slowly decays over time.

“By default it is a very good staggering weapon. Okay damage, good at dealing with hordes. But once it goes on fire, it is also going to apply Burn stacks. So you control the horde and slowly burn them down.” ~ Matteo

Several of the talents in the Sister’s tree rely on burning enemies, so this weapon pairs well with those. But you will be able to read more about that in our next Feature Highlight!

Beyond applying Burn, this meter can also be consumed for the Weapon’s Special. At the cost of one third of the gauge, the Sister can release a cloud of smoke around her, which applies some Burn and also obscures line of sight for enemy gunners for a few seconds!

The Mace of the Righteous is a weapon typically given to Novitiates who have distinguished themselves, earning the honour to wield this holy weapon and be an exemplar to her sisters on the battlefield.

On the opposite end of the spectrum, we have…

Penitent Eviscerator

eviscerator1920×1059 731 KB

This particularly large Eviscerator is typically found in the hands of Sisters that need to atone for a sin, by jumping into the fray and decimating the enemies of the Imperiumin brutal carnage. While our Sisters Novitiate are not Repentia, we still wanted to enable part of this player fantasy through this weapon (as well as some talents!)

This Eviscerator presents a new take on chain weapons! While this blade still has that iconic feel of sawing through enemies, we wanted it to feel less punishing to use.

“For this weapon, you shouldn’t be scared to get stuck in “sticky” attacks, but we still wanted to maintain the gruesome feel of you sawing through the flesh.” ~ Erik

The Light Attacks are strikedown attacks, perfect for dealing high single target damage, which stick and saw through opponents they hit.

The Heavy Attacks are wide sweeps with high cleave and only stick to an enemy once the swing gets stopped (usually by hitting enough enemies or by striking a sufficiently tough Elite).

For these normal attacks, even when the weapon sticks to an enemy, it does not leave you vulnerable for long.

As for the Weapon Special, instead of being an Activated Special like most other chain weapons, it is a unique attack. The Sister holds the back of the blade and swings horizontally in a brutal, sawing attack, dealing heavy damage to anything it sticks to. When chaining into it, the direction of the attack will depend on the previous swing, seamlessly fitting into the chain.

“If you click the Special, you start revving and charging the attack, and you hit on release. You can also tap it to do a quicker one, or you can hold it to rev it up and do more damage.” ~ Erik

Novitiate Blade and Shield

shield1920×1059 496 KB

This is Darktide’s first Sword & Shield weapon! A knightly weapon that provides excellent defense, allowing you to block otherwise unblockable attacks (such as Crusher Overheads) as well as ranged fire from the front.

The shield is used not just to block, but is also employed in some of the attacks in this weapon’s moveset.

“The shield is more of a horde control, staggering tool, while the sword can be used for stabs or swings to do damage, both on multiple enemies or single targets. It is not the most damaging type of weapon, it is more defensive, but it is still good enough to deal with different types of enemies.” ~ Matteo

This weapon has two marks, which perform similarly to each other but have different movesets and levels of complexity, so the choice is more about the preference and comfort of each player.

A unique feature of this weapon is that the Heavy attack wind ups hold the shield in front and actually block attacks even while charging the strike, keeping the user safe while attacking.

As mentioned in our previous blog, the Sister Novitiate class will have features around a new resource called Faith, and this weapon interacts with that resource as part of its Weapon Special.

Pressing the Weapon Special button will consume a portion of your Faith to bless the shield for a good duration. This will improve all aspects of the shield, reducing the stamina cost to block, strengthening the shield bashes, and increasing the range of the Pushes.

Novitiate Blade

You can also wield the Novitiate Blade without the shield! While it is the same sword, the stand alone Novitiate Blade is its own weapon, with unique stats and moveset.

“We wanted to use this weapon to showcase the martial skills of the Novitiates. They’re extremely well trained soldiers, so they will display proper swordsmanship techniques.” ~ Matteo

To do this, we took inspiration from another knightly blade from Warhammer: Vermintide 2, the Bretonnian Longsword! Similar to it, the wind up animation of the Heavy Attacks will have the Sister hold the blade above in a familiar protective stance, blocking melee enemy strikes.

To synergize with this mechanic, the Novitiate Blade has an innate buff whenever blocking an attack that gives the next strike a significant damage buff. While this triggers from any blocks, the duration is short enough that it will generally mostly affect the ‘riposte’ from immediately releasing the Heavy Attack after blocking with the wind up.

This weapon also has two modes! When starting a mission the blade will be in Vanguard mode, and all of the Heavy attacks will be wide swings to clear hordes. Pressing the Weapon Special during the wind up of a Heavy Attack will release a heavy thrust and switch the weapon to a more Strikedown focused mode that features lots of thrusts and stabs.

Similarly, to switch back to Vanguard mode, you must press the Weapon Special while winding up a Heavy Attack in Strikedown mode.

While both movesets are relatively simple to use, mastering the art of switching seamlessly to the correct style on the fly adds a lot of depth to this weapon.

Similar to the Novitiate Blade and Shield, pressing the Weapon Special when not charging a Heavy Attack will have the Novitiate spend some Faith to bless the blade, improving its damage and overall performance, while also making it particularly deadly to Infested and Unyielding enemy types.

Just like with the original Bretonnian Longsword, our animators took inspiration from real, historical European Martial Arts to bring this weapon to life, taking special care to make the Novitiate’s swordsmanship shine through in an authentic way.

Hand Flamer

handflamer1920×1037 632 KB

Flamers in general are an iconic tool that the Sisters use to purge heretics and corruption, and so we wanted the Novitiates to arrive with a brand new one!

“Compared to the regular Flamer, it has much higher mobility. It is much faster to pull out and shoot, reload, and to generally move around with it. The disadvantage is that it is slightly less powerful and has a smaller magazine.” ~ Matteo

Similar to the existing Flamers, the Primary Fire shoots a single burst of flame with good stagger potential, while the Secondary Fire allows the user to shoot a continuous stream of flame.

For the Weapon Special, the Novitiate can overcharge the weapon, entering a special stance that shoots much stronger flames at the cost of doubling the amount of ammo consumed.

“If you need to deal with bigger enemies and apply Burn as fast as possible, you can go into this mode.” ~ Matteo

Entering this mode is very fast, so it is easy to tap into this enhanced, but costly firing mode at a moment’s notice.

Overall, the Hand Flamer is a more compact version of the Flamer in every way, trading some damage and flame length in exchange for smoother handling, faster reloads, and overall just being much snappier when switching into or out of it. Less committal, more versatile!

Condemnor Stakethrower

stakethrower1920×1037 584 KB

An iconic weapon for Novitiates specifically, this is a semi-automatic crossbow that shoots silver stakes, the ideal weapon to dispatch heretical Psykers!

This is an accurate, but relatively slow firing weapon, perfect for sniping key targets at long distances. It deals solid damage to all targets, even without hitting weak spots. It is efficient, accurate, and satisfying to use.

The Primary Fire shoots a bolt from the hip, remaining mostly accurate but still suffering from some spread and recoil when compared to the Secondary Fire, which braces the weapon.

For its Weapon Special, this is another weapon that relies on Faith! Pressing the Weapon Special button consumes a small amount of Faith to bless the topmost bolt in the magazine, dealing extra damage and applying Sanctification to targets struck by the holy bolt. For more information on the Sanctification effect, look forwards to the next Feature Highlight!

Each magazine holds a generous amount of bolts, which you can visually see on the weapon! While the total ammo count is not too limited, players should still avoid being wasteful with the shots if they don’t want to run out of bolts too quickly.

Finally, this weapon has another unique feature…

Enemy-pinning bolts

Killing enemies standing near parts of the environment, like a wall or a door, has a chance to leave them pinned to it by the stake! While this feature does not impact gameplay much, it is a really cool detail that we’re really proud of and wanted to show off! To do that, we’ve interviewed Daniel, who was involved in the code behind it.

“I’m Daniel, I’m a Gameplay Programmer in the Class team. I mostly do Ability work or Talent work. I help out on Weapons and a bit of everything, but mostly Talents. I’ve been working on the pinning mechanic with the help of our Engine team to be able to do some Cool Crossbow Stuff!” ~ Daniel

This is a feature inspired from Warhammer: Vermintide 1, where projectiles from bows and crossbows could pin enemies to walls and such! The implementation here is much different, however, avoiding some of the issues that the original Vermintide feature had while also giving us some cool effects that are unique to the Darktide implementation.

“We wanted it to be based on the Physics of the engine so that there would be no issues when sticking things to something like doors or platforms that could move. So we needed a system that would interact with the existing physics of the game.” ~ Daniel

How it works is that when an enemy is killed by a stake from the Novitiate’s Stakethrower, the body part that was struck by the killing blow can attach to a piece of terrain if it comes into contact with it during a short timeframe, with the stake protruding from the wound.

This relies on the natural blowback already present in the ragdoll system when an enemy unit dies, and a lot of fine tuning went into the system to get it to feel satisfying without looking too unrealistic.

“The tricky part was controlling when to do the pinning and when not to do it. […] It’s also not something that’s intended to happen 90% of the time, it’s something that happens 10, maybe 20% of the time, when you kill someone who’s near a wall and such. It’s a cool detail more than a defining feature.” ~ Daniel

The pinned body parts are also attached with a certain level of strength to it, which means that it’s able to break if something pulls hard enough on it!

“For example (and I’ve tried this many times and it’s really fun, hehe), if you pin both arms, one on each half of a door, when it opens they break free.” ~ Daniel

You can also shoot at a corpse to add more stakes and pin the enemy from different body parts even when it’s already dead. Fun!

We tried a lot of variations to see what fit best, refining with each iteration until we arrived at the version that will launch. Some of the versions we tried included pinning corpses to another corpse, or pinning dead enemies to living ones, but that brought its own host of issues.

“When I tried that with two Ogryns, they started to spin around, like two planets orbiting each other. They started to float and spin faster and faster in every direction, then eventually they got so fast that they disintegrated - By which I mean they travelled out of bounds in a single frame and got deleted.” ~ Daniel

Last, but not least…

Meltagun

melta replacement1920×1080 394 KB

A fan favorite and a longtime request from the community, the Meltagun is arriving to Darktide! This weapon will be available both to the Sister Novitiate and the Veteran classes, and is a free addition that does not require purchase of the DLC to access.

“The Meltagun is one of the most iconic weapons in Warhammer 40,000 and it’s been a lot of fun making it for Darktide, but it’s also been a challenge because there’s a lot of expectations behind it.” ~ Erik

This devastating weapon fires a beam of superheated energy that vaporizes anything in its path, destroying flesh and armour alike. While it is cumbersome to wield, it brings a level of firepower that is unmatched by anything we’ve had before on Darktide.

The heat from a Meltagun’s beam is so powerful that enemies standing near it will be set on fire even if they’re not directly hit by it!

Said beam rapidly loses power with distance, however, so while it can be used against targets in mid-range if needed, the precious ammunition is best saved for disintegrating enemies in close range, where the damage is at its best. Speaking of, players should be considerate of their ammo, as both the magazine and the total ammo reserves of this weapon are quite limited.

Beyond just the damage and the impressive visual and sound effects of the Meltagun, we wanted to represent the power of it in its handling! For instance, while firing a beam, turning your aim is much harder and your movement is slowed.

“It both feels and acts heavy.” ~ Erik

When firing this weapon you will commit to a beam that lasts for a fixed duration for each point of ammunition spent. The Primary Fire will have players shooting from the hip, while the Secondary Fire will be a braced fire that slows you down further but has more control over the beam, and it will fire for slightly longer per shot.

For its Weapon Special, a simple, quick bash with the heavy weapon, great for getting enemies off of you before swapping to your melee weapon if you get surrounded.

Bonus Questions!

Which of these weapons is your favorite?

“All of the weapons are really cool, I think this is a super complete package, both for melee and ranged. […] I’m gonna say the Melta was probably the most fun to work on. I think the team did a really good job, it looks great, sounds great, and I think it’s fun to play. For the melee ones, the Novitiate Blade is probably my favorite ‘cause… It’s awesome! I really love the Heavy Attack blocks, and it looks and feels great to play with. But I like playing with all of these weapons!” ~ Erik

“For me, I’ll say the Novitiate Blade and the Stakethrower. They’re both very cool and are unique to Darktide and… they’re just fun to play with!” ~ Matteo

What was different about working on these weapons?

“We wanted to use Faith in the weapons more directly. So we have the Novitiate Blade, the Novitiate Blade & Shield, and the Stakethrower that have something that uses Faith directly. But also generally, these are the types of weapon that we want to convey the Sister Novitiates’ uniqueness with.” ~ Matteo

“The Faith and the Uniqueness was a fun aspect. […] It feels like everything in this kit has a very special intent and purpose.” ~ Erik

That leads us to the end of this blog, that’s all for today!
